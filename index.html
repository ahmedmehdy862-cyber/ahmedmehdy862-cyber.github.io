My Approach

How I think before I design

A.M

Ahmed Mahdy

Graphic Designer · Social Media

I start by understanding the purpose behind the project, then develop a clear visual direction before moving into execution. Every detail is considered to create work that is consistent, purposeful, and built to communicate.

  1. Think

    Understand the goal, the audience and the message before a single shape is drawn.

  2. Concept

    Shape a clear visual direction that the whole project can follow.

  3. Design

    Execute with precision and intent — every detail earns its place.

  4. Refine

    Review, polish and align until the work stays consistent and clear.

I care about design as a tool for communication — not just a visual shape.
